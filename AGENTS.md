[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the single
[project knowledge skill](../.agents/skills/deepai-project-knowledge/SKILL.md)
before planning, implementation or testing. They carry accepted decisions,
parallel-work ownership and memory/vault completion guidance. A separately
opened component does not automatically discover the parent skill.

source and runbooks and report the unavailable shared context. Do not create a
replacement vault or duplicate skill. Component instructions remain scoped to
this component; the parent policy does not expand a task's external permissions.
